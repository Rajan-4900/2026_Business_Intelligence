What the check does not cover

  • Existing similar places count as evidence for feasibility; the check does not consider saturation. See Section 5 for competition.
  • Rent, start-up cost, licences, staffing and actual demand are not part of the check.
  • A "Feasible" result means the location passes the platform's rules. It is not an investment recommendation.

Conditions to satisfy before investing

  • Customer demand confirmed by your own footfall counts or survey.
  • Rent and running costs that the conservative revenue scenario can cover.
  • All required licences and approvals confirmed in writing.
  • A clear reason customers would choose you over the competitors in Section 5.
