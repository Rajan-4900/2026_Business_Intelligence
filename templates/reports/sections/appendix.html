  • Revenue engine for "{{ s.revenue.business_type }}": average spend {{ s.revenue.assumptions.avg_spend|inr }}, conversion {{ s.revenue.assumptions.conversion_rate|pct }}, ideal {{ s.revenue.assumptions.optimal_daily_customers.0 }}–{{ s.revenue.assumptions.optimal_daily_customers.1 }} customers per day{% if s.revenue.uses_default_assumptions %} (generic defaults – no category-specific values){% endif %}.

    • {% endif %}
  • Distances are straight-line (not walking or driving).

Limitations

  • No pedestrian footfall, sales, rent, cost or income data is available. Map features stand in for activity.
  • OpenStreetMap coverage varies; missing or outdated entries change every count in this report.
  • The ML model is small (72 examples) and has not been validated against real business outcomes.
  • Competitor matching relies on category names and misses differently tagged places.
  • Revenue figures are gross sales estimates. They are not profit and not guaranteed.
  • AI-written text was checked automatically for figures not in the data, but you should still read it critically.
